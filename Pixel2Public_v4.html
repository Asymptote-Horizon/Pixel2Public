04 / The venture story

From a flat design
to a street-ready decision.

Pixel2Public helps outdoor advertising teams pressure-test creative work in context before committing to physical production.

Venture Pixel to PerfectProgram Wadhwani Ignite
The problem

A beautiful design can fail on the street.

Designers create on a flat 2D screen. Once a billboard is printed and mounted, distance, viewing angle, glare, rain, traffic and physical obstructions can change what people actually see.

“We can approve the artwork on a laptop. But we cannot confidently predict how it will read from the road.”
Flat 2D proofingManual mockupsLate-stage rework
The solution

Preview the campaign in its intended context.

Upload an existing design, choose a Pune planning zone and explore how it may appear under different lighting and weather conditions. Use the preview to spot potential issues earlier and improve the client conversation.

“Pixel2Public turns a static artwork into a contextual decision-support preview.”
Artwork uploadLocation contextVisibility checks
Who it serves

Built around the outdoor creative workflow.

The primary customer is a senior graphic designer or art director at an outdoor advertising agency. Agency teams can use the platform to review work, communicate trade-offs and prepare more persuasive client presentations.

Creative agenciesOOH media plannersBrand teams
What exists today

A functional front-end concept prototype.

This demo includes image upload, sample campaigns, interactive location selection, a stylised street preview, lighting and rain controls, heuristic visibility indicators and a savings calculator.

Advanced computer vision, true 3D reconstruction, live weather, verified billboard inventory and operator booking integrations are future development work—not connected in this prototype.
